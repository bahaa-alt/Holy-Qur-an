Counts and results here are WORD POSITIONS, not segments. An Arabic word can be several segments — a prefixed lām of emphasis and the noun it attaches to are two — so a word carrying two tagged segments appears once. That is the same unit the query page uses, which is what lets one query combine a root with a grammatical feature.

The function tags are not “occurrences” in this app's usual sense. An occurrence is a segment carrying a root, and 15,413 of the 17,014 tagged segments here are rootless particles. They are a parallel layer, counted separately, and they never affect a root's totals.

Passive voice is tagged in the syntactic layer rather than as a grammatical category, because voice is independent of aspect — a passive perfect verb is still a perfect verb, and is still counted as one everywhere else in this app.
