Root

ص د ق

/Sdq/

51% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root S-D-Q primarily means to speak the truth, to be veracious, or to prove true. It encompasses sincerity in words, actions, and intentions, contrasting with falsehood.
