Root

ص ل و

/Slw/

40% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root صلو (Slw) primarily means to pray, supplicate, or perform the ritual prayer (ṣalāh). When God or angels "pray" for someone, it means to bless, have mercy, or magnify them.
