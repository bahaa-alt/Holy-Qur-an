Root

ف ع ل

/fEl/

58% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root فِعْل (fiʿl) refers to a deed, action, or motion, encompassing any origination or effect produced by an agent. It's a broad term for what has become apparent due to a motive.
