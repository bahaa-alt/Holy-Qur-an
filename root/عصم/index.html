Root

ع ص م

/ESm/

38% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root عِصَامٌ (ʿiṣām) refers to a tie or cord, like those used for water-skins or bags, to secure their contents or for carrying. It also denotes anything that provides protection or preservation.
