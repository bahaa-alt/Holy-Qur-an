Root

ع د و

/Edw/

44% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root Edw relates to hostility, transgression, and overstepping bounds. Al-ʿadāwah is enmity of the heart, while al-ʿudwān is transgression in action. It also carries the sense of running and rushing (ʿadw).
