Root

ن ع م

/nEm/

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root نعم (nEm) primarily signifies a benefit, favor, or blessing from God, encompassing boons, bounties, and grace. It also denotes ease, plenty, and welfare, often contrasted with hardship.
