ي و م

/ywm/

75% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root "ywm" primarily refers to a period of time, often a day from sunrise to sunset, or from dawn to sunset. It can also denote an event or accident, a day's journey, or a battle/conflict.
