Root

ب ع د

/bEd/

55% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root بَعُدَ primarily means to be or become distant, remote, or far off. It can also signify perishing or dying. In some contexts, it expresses improbability or reaching an extreme degree.
