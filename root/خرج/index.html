Root

خ ر ج

/khrj/

56% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root خَرَجَ (khrj) primarily means to go or come out, to issue forth. The form أَخْرَجَ (akhraja) means to make something go out, to expel, extract, or produce it.
