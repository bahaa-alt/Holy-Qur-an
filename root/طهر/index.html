ط ه ر

/Thr/

23% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root طهر (Thr) primarily means to cleanse or purify oneself, often with water, like performing ablution. It can also metaphorically mean to remove oneself from impurities, sins, or ignoble habits.
