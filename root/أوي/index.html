Root

أ و ي

/Awy/

53% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root أوي primarily means to betake oneself to a place for lodging, shelter, or refuge. It implies seeking protection or a dwelling, often with the sense of returning or resorting to a safe haven.
