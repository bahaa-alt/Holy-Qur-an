Root

و ل د

/wld/

54% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root "wld" primarily refers to birth and offspring. A "walīd" is a newborn, infant, or young child. It can also denote a boy, youth, or servant, especially one born into servitude.
