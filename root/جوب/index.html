Root

ج و ب

/jwb/

77% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root جوب (jwb) primarily means to make a hole, cut, or hollow out something, like cutting a rock or a garment. It also extends to traversing or cutting through a space, such as a land or darkness.
