Root

ع ل م

/Elm/

58% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root "Elm" (علم) primarily means "to know" or "to be acquainted with." It signifies true, certain knowledge, often implying a deeper understanding than mere acquaintance.
