Root

خ و ف

/khwf/

62% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root خ و ف primarily means "to fear" or "be afraid." It can also mean "to know" or "to think," as seen in Quranic verses like "if a woman fears ill-treatment from her husband.
