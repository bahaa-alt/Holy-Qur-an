Root

ع م ل

/Eml/

61% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root عَمَل (Eml) refers to an intentional action or deed, often implying effort or difficulty. It's more specific than a general "act" (فِعْل) and is typically attributed to animate beings.
