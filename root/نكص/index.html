Root

ن ك ص

/nkS/

50% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root نكص (nkS) means to recede, retire, or draw back from something, often due to awe or fear. It can also mean to turn back from something one had applied themselves to, especially a good thing.
