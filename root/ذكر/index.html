Root

ذ ك ر

/dhkr/

74% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root ذَكَرَ (dhakara) primarily means to remember or recall something, especially after forgetting. It also encompasses the act of mentioning, telling, or relating something verbally.
