Root

ق ت ل

/qtl/

22% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root Q-T-L, beyond its common meaning of "to kill," can also mean "to know something thoroughly." It implies a deep, complete understanding, as if one has mastered the knowledge of that thing.
