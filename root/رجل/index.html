Root

ر ج ل

/rjl/

60% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root "رجل" (rijl) primarily refers to the leg or foot of humans and animals. It can also denote a part or portion of something, like half of a roasted sheep or a pair of saddlebags.
