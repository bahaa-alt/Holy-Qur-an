Root

ر ح م

/rHm/

67% Meccan

Meaning (after Lane's Lexicon): The root rHm centers on mercy, compassion, and the womb (raHim). Al-raHmah is a tenderness that moves one to do good to another. When attributed to God, it denotes pure grace and favor. Al-raHmān and al-raHīm are intensive forms expressing all-encompassing and specific mercy.
